# Instructions: email-triage

*This file is rewritten periodically by `scripts/consolidate_learnings.py` from the
raw entries in `learnings/email-rejections.md`. It should stay a lean, current set of
rules — not a growing log. Hand-edit freely; the consolidation job will merge future
learnings into whatever's here.*

## Importance classification

Starting rules (no learnings yet to consolidate — update as patterns emerge):

- **Important:** anything requiring a reply, a decision, or containing a deadline —
  personal correspondence, anything from an instructor/advisor, anything about the
  NASA/CLEAR project, anything with a due date or time-sensitive ask.
- **Not important:** automated notifications, marketing/promotional mail,
  newsletters, receipts with no action needed, mailing-list digests.
- **When unsure:** classify as important and draft a short, low-commitment reply (or
  flag for read-only review) rather than silently skipping it. Silence on a real
  email is worse than one extra item in the queue.

## Voice / tone

No samples logged yet. Default to: concise, direct, no filler pleasantries, no
corporate-speak. Sign off simply. Revisit once a few approved/edited drafts give
real signal.

## Things not to do

- Do not draft replies to threads that are purely informational with no ask (e.g.
  "FYI" mail, calendar notifications) — flag as important-but-no-draft if they need
  attention, otherwise skip.
- Do not guess at commitments (dates, dollar amounts, "yes I'll do X") the user
  hasn't actually made.
